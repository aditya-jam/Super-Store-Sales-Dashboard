🏆 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard** built using Superstore sales data from **2011–2014** to help management quickly understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, and customer behavior.

🎯 Business Objective

Transform raw sales data into actionable insights that help answer:

* How are sales and profit performing?
* Which products and categories perform best or worst?
* Which regions and states generate the most sales?
* Which customers and segments are most valuable?

 📊 Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview

* Total Sales, Orders, Profit & Profit Margin
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Sales by Category
* Profit by Region
* Year Filter
* Key Business Insights

2. Product Performance

* Top 5 Products by Sales
* Top/Low Product & Category analysis using **Bookmarks**
* Total Quantity & Product KPIs
* Average Discount & Profitability
* Key Insights

3. Customer & Regional Analysis

* Total Customers
* Average Orders/Sales/Profit per Customer
* Sales by Region
* Sales by State
* Customer & Segment analysis using **Bookmarks**
* Key Insights

### 🛠️ Tools & Skills

**Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | Time Intelligence | Bookmarks | Data Visualization | Business Storytelling**

### 💡 Key Findings

* **Technology** generated the highest sales.
* **Copiers** generated the highest profit.
* **Tables** was identified as a loss-making category.
* **West** was the strongest region.
* **California** led state-level sales.
* **Consumer** was the largest customer segment.

### 🚀 Business Value

The dashboard converts thousands of transaction records into a **simple, interactive BI solution** that enables management to identify opportunities, investigate underperforming areas, and make faster data-driven decisions.
